# T-015: Weekly raffle

Status: backlog
Blocked by: T-009

## What to build

Crabada's Lucky Draw, rebuilt with sound funding and sound randomness.

Weekly raffle with rare-creature or CLAM prizes funded from fee revenue (never minted).
Tickets cost Doubloons (sink) or CLAM (revenue); winner selection uses commit-reveal randomness, never blockhash.
Requires a gambling-law legality check per target jurisdiction before shipping.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Draw is verifiably fair and reproducible from on-chain data
- [ ] Prizes capped by the funded pool
- [ ] Legal sign-off documented before mainnet enablement
