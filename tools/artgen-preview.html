Gen-0 Art: Reef Arcade and two new takes

Same five creatures, same seeds and trait rolls in every row. After the 2026-08-14 evening pass: the turtle is the original website drawing, ported unchanged with its small eye (trait eyes scale down to match); the seahorse is redrawn from a real seahorse silhouette reference - crest, blunt snout, pectoral fins, belly plates and the signature spiral tail - with the mouth trait at the snout tip. Night Dive and Pastel Plush are the same system wearing different skins.
