Whitepaper

Pearl Street: an honest economy for an undersea market.

v0.3 · August 2026 · pre-launch draft

CHAPTER I

Vision

Millions of people check their portfolio the way other people check the weather. Pearl Street turns that daily ritual into a game: an undersea Wall Street where creature crews sail trading voyages keyed to real equities, and where the market's every move - rallies, dumps, earnings storms - becomes gameplay.

We build on Robinhood Chain, an Ethereum Layer 2 purpose-built for tokenized equities, with sub-second blocks, negligible fees, and live Chainlink price feeds for real stocks. It is a chain with real users and real dollars - and, until Pearl Street, no games at all.

CHAPTER II

The lesson we refuse to repeat

Our genre's most famous ancestor let players mint its reward token endlessly against playtime. Every sink depended on new players arriving; when growth stalled, emissions kept compounding and the token fell for a year straight. By the end it had four tradable, inflating currencies - four sell buttons - and no floor under any of them.

Minting never created profit. It created tokens. The dollars always came from the next buyer.

Pearl Street is designed from that autopsy. The average player is a net spender because playing is fun - the Fortnite half of our economy. The money that flows out to winners is money other players put in, redistributed through competition with a protocol rake - the poker half. Nothing tradable is minted against gameplay, ever.

CHAPTER III

The reef and its five houses

Every creature belongs to one of five species, and species is class: the Octopus (strategist), the Shark (raider), the Turtle (defender), the Seahorse (courier), and the Pufferfish (tank). A balanced crew mixes houses; a specialist crew leans into one playstyle and lives with its weaknesses.

Voyages. Parties of three sail fixed-length expeditions keyed to a real ticker. Settlement reads the ticker's Chainlink feed: the signed price move applies a bounded multiplier to a guaranteed base haul. Sector affinity between crew and ticker strengthens the multiplier.

Energy. Every creature has a fixed daily energy budget - our anti-botting spine. No wallet can out-grind the cap, and industrial farms gain nothing from running around the clock.

Looting. Active voyages are raidable during a vulnerability window. Combat resolves on party stats with two rounds of reinforcements and an underdog's-revenge chance for defenders. Only base hauls are stealable, and each voyage can be raided once.

The Tavern. Idle creatures hire out as battle reinforcements at owner-set prices; the house takes a rake.

Breeding. Supply grows only within per-season population caps, with escalating costs and lifetime breed limits. Old generations retire into cosmetic relics.

Seasons. Leaderboards follow market quarters. Earnings weeks are in-game events. The market calendar is a content pipeline nobody has to build.

CHAPTER IV

The three currencies

CLAM - the money. A fully-reserved wrapper over USDG. Mint by depositing USDG (2% fee); redeem back anytime (5% fee); every fee goes to the prize pool. The vault enforces reserve = supply at all times, redemption cannot be paused by anyone, no function can withdraw the reserve, and the reserve is never lent or deployed. Proof of backing is one public RPC call.

Doubloons - the grind. Earned by playing, bounded by energy, soulbound forever. Doubloons buy training, upgrades, breeding discounts and cosmetics. Because they cannot be transferred, they absorb play-to-earn pressure without creating a single unit of sell pressure.

PEARL - the upside. The only speculative asset, launched only after the game generates revenue. Hard-capped. Per-epoch emissions are bounded by trailing protocol revenue: no revenue, no emission. A fixed share of revenue fills an on-chain reserve against which PEARL can always be burned at floor - a floor that can only rise. Staking PEARL earns fee-share and Doubloons, never a new uncapped token.

CHAPTER V

Where the money flows

  • In: Gen-0 and breeding mints, CLAM wrap and unwrap fees, marketplace fees, tavern rake, loot insurance premiums, team-slot purchases, cosmetics and season passes.
  • Out: season prize pools, raffle prizes, tournament pots - all paid in CLAM, all capped by what revenue actually funded.
  • Never: emissions of anything tradable against playtime. The printer does not exist.

Because fees sit on money flow rather than player growth, the protocol earns at any population size - the failure mode where sinks die with growth is structurally removed.

CHAPTER VI

The chain, and why it matters

  • Robinhood Chain is an Arbitrum-stack Ethereum L2 with ~0.1s blocks and fees measured in fractions of a cent - game-grade UX with Ethereum settlement.
  • Live Chainlink feeds for dozens of major equities give us tamper-resistant market data on-chain; voyages settle against the feed, staleness-checked, with a safe base-haul fallback.
  • First-come-first-served sequencing means loot raids cannot be sniped by gas auctions - a genuine fairness upgrade for PvP.
  • Account abstraction is native: players connect, wrap USDG, and play with sponsored gas. No ETH required, no seed-phrase hazing.
  • Pearl Street reads stock prices only. The game never holds, distributes, or touches tokenized securities.

And the market itself is stakeable. Because Pearl Street lives on the chain where equities are ERC-20s, staking them into the game is a native feature, not an integration. That deserves its own chapter.

CHAPTER VII

The Berths: stake the market itself

What a Stock Token actually is. Robinhood Chain's flagship assets are Stock Tokens: real US equities and ETFs carried on-chain as standard ERC-20 tokens - NVDA, AAPL, SPY and roughly ninety more - issued by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited. Legally they are tokenized debt instruments that track the underlying stock's economics; they confer price exposure, not shareholder rights. Dividends fold straight into the token through an on-chain multiplier (the ERC-8056 scaled-amount standard), so a Stock Token tracks the stock's total return and still trades like any other token on the chain. They are securities with real-world restrictions: not offered to US persons, with further limits in Canada, the UK, and Switzerland.

How Pearl Street uses them - two strictly separated roles:

  • As prices, for everyone. The core game consumes Chainlink price feeds for the 35 feed-covered tickers. Voyages settle against the number, never the asset: the game contracts never hold, sell, or distribute a Stock Token, and nobody needs to own one to play anything.
  • As positions, for eligible players. Where Stock Tokens are legally available, holders can put their actual portfolio to work in the game through Berths. This layer is optional, region-gated, and additive; the Gen-0 sale and the core loop stand entirely without it.

A Berth is a mooring on your creature's sector: deposit eligible Stock Tokens (the feed-covered tickers), and your position becomes part of your crew's strength. Your actual NVDA powers your actual Tech crew.

How a Berth grows you:

  • Deeper affinity, bigger share. The sector affinity bonus scales from the base +10% up to a hard cap of +25% with the value berthed (square-root curve, so skill stays ahead of capital). A stronger multiplier means a larger share of every daily pool release and a faster climb up the season leaderboard - the boost compounds voyage after voyage.
  • Commitment is the strategy. Berth power ramps in over 5 days of sustained position and unstaking resets the ramp. Long-term conviction, not epoch-hopping capital, is what the game rewards - a berth is a position statement, visible on your crew.
  • Your position keeps being yours. Berthed tokens keep their full total return: dividends and splits accrue automatically through the token's on-chain multiplier while they sit in the berth. The game adds a layer on top of your portfolio; it never takes a slice of it.
  • The Houses. Every berth counts toward its sector's House - Tech, Energy, Finance and the rest - and each season the Houses are ranked by total berthed value. House standings unlock event modifiers, cosmetics, and bragging rights. Sector loyalty becomes a team sport played with real conviction.
  • Seats on the exchange. Opening berths requires locking PEARL - a seat on Pearl Street, in the oldest tradition of exchanges. More seats, more berths.

The guarantees, same as everywhere on Pearl Street:

  • Withdrawals can never be paused, by anyone, for any reason. No function exists that can move berthed tokens anywhere but back to their owner.
  • If the Berths are ever retired, retirement blocks new deposits and ends boosts - withdrawals stay open until the last token has left.
  • Berths never mint anything. They change your share of the capped pool release; the no-printer rule holds even here.
  • Per-token deposit caps and issuer-upgrade monitoring contain the risks we do not control.

Stock Tokens are unavailable in some jurisdictions, so Berths are a feature for eligible regions; nothing in the Gen-0 sale depends on them.

CHAPTER VIII

The Gen-0 sale

One founding generation of 7,500 creatures - 1,500 per species - minted in CLAM at 100 CLAM (80 CLAM allowlist), capped at 15 per wallet, never re-issued. Gen-0 creatures are the only creatures that will ever exist without breeding, and early supply governs everything downstream. Terms are planned values; final terms are published at mint.

The pool cannot go dry, by construction. The season prize pool releases at most 1.5% of its balance per day - 80% to voyage hauls, 20% to the season-end leaderboard pot - and 100% of all ongoing protocol fees flow back into it. Every reward is a share of something real; more players means smaller shares, never a broken promise.

Use of proceeds. At least 30% of the Gen-0 raise funds Season One's prize pool directly. The remainder funds development, marketing, and the treasury reserve, held in the published operations wallet with a published budget. The exact split is published before the sale opens and is verifiable on-chain afterward.

The Founder's Wake. Gen-0 creatures carry a permanent founder bonus on PEARL rewards once PEARL exists - earned through play from the capped rewards allocation, vested like all PEARL rewards. It is a bonus on playing, never an airdrop for buying; final mechanics are published with the PEARL launch.

The art is stored on-chain, forever. Every Gen-0 creature is deterministic SVG rendered by the contract itself, in the tradition Nouns pioneered - the next chapter explains the system in full.

PEARL is not part of the Gen-0 sale. It arrives a few days after the game is live, through a short public pre-sale whose proceeds mostly seed locked PEARL/USDG liquidity - it arrives after revenue does, and that ordering is a feature, not a delay.

CHAPTER IX

On-chain art, the Nouns way

Nouns changed what an NFT can be by making the blockchain itself the artist: the artwork is not a link to a server, it is code in the contract, and the chain draws it on demand. Pearl Street adopts that architecture for every creature it will ever mint.

  • The art is a program, not a file. Every Gen-0 creature is pure SVG composed from trait layers - species base, body-color tier, eyes, headgear, mouth, aura, and sector tag. The renderer lives on-chain and reproduces the art generator byte-for-byte; the token's metadata call returns the complete image with no IPFS, no image server, no link that can rot.
  • Rarity is verifiable, not promised. Trait odds are fixed in the contract - four body tiers from Biolume at 55% down to Coral at 5%, five eye styles, six kinds of headgear, and the rest - and every creature is generated deterministically from its commit-reveal seed. Anyone can re-derive any creature from its seed and check the odds against the code.
  • Nothing can be swapped after you buy. Because image and metadata are computed on-chain from the seed, no admin can point your token at different art later. What you reveal is what you own, for as long as the chain produces blocks.
  • What we borrow, and what we do not. We borrow Nouns' art architecture: fully on-chain storage and rendering with a transparent trait system. We do not copy its one-per-day auction or DAO treasury model; Pearl Street's mint, seasons, and economy follow the design in the chapters above.

The creatures on this page are the working prototype of exactly this system, drawn by the same deterministic generator the contract will implement. Final art is locked and published before mint; the guarantee that it lives on-chain is locked already.

CHAPTER X

PEARL: what the token actually does

PEARL is not a promise of profit bolted onto a game; it is the working capital asset of the exchange, with five concrete jobs:

  • Seats. Locking PEARL opens Berths (Chapter VII). The endgame feature of the whole platform runs through the token, taking supply out of circulation as adoption grows.
  • Fee share. Staked PEARL earns a share of protocol fee revenue - real CLAM from the vault toll, marketplace, tavern, and insurance - plus Doubloons. Never a new uncapped token; the CRAM mistake is not coming back.
  • The floor. A fixed share of revenue fills an on-chain reserve, and anyone can burn PEARL at floor value (reserve divided by circulating supply). The floor can only rise; every panic-seller below it raises it for everyone else.
  • Scarcity by construction. Hard cap of 1,000,000,000. Reward emissions each epoch are bounded by trailing protocol revenue - no revenue, no emission. PEARL cannot be farmed into oblivion because gameplay does not print it.
  • The Founder's Wake. Gen-0 creatures earn a +20% bonus on play-earned PEARL, forever - the founding generation's stake in the token era.

The launch pledge. A few days after the game is live, PEARL launches through a single short public pre-sale - and this whitepaper fixes how every unit of the proceeds is used, in advance:

  • 75% into the PEARL liquidity pool, with the LP tokens timelocked for a minimum of 24 months. The lock address is published; anyone can verify the liquidity cannot be pulled.
  • 25% to operations - development, marketing, security - through the same published operations wallet as the Gen-0 raise, with a published budget.
  • No insider discount. The launch event price is the listing price, with per-wallet caps and vesting on large allocations. Team allocations vest longest.
  • The throttle still holds. The launch event changes how PEARL arrives, not how it lives: emissions stay bounded by trailing revenue, and the redemption floor still only rises.

This ordering - game first, revenue first, then a token whose liquidity is locked by construction - is the exact inverse of the launches that killed our genre. We put it in the whitepaper so you can hold us to it.

CHAPTER XI

Risks, plainly stated

  • No game economy can make every player profitable. Skilled and consistent players win money that casual players spent. If a game promises otherwise, it is lying to you.
  • CLAM's DEX price can float between the redeem floor (about $0.95) and the mint ceiling (about $1.02); the vault, not any pool, is the source of truth.
  • Robinhood Chain has permissioned validators and a sequencer operated with compliance screening; the chain's trust model is disclosed in full in our documentation.
  • Chainlink feeds can pause or go stale during corporate actions; voyages settle to base hauls when data is not fresh.
  • Berthed Stock Tokens are held by a game contract, and all Stock Tokens share one issuer-controlled upgrade mechanism outside our control. We contain this with per-token deposit caps and monitoring, and berth withdrawals can never be paused - but the underlying issuer risk is real and is yours to weigh. Stock Tokens are unavailable in some jurisdictions.
  • Smart contracts carry risk. The CLAM vault ships only after external audit, and its invariants are publicly testable.
