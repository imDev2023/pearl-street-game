Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: -

## Question
Is legal sign-off required for the CLAM wrapper, the PEARL pre-sale, or Founder claims?

## Answer
No legal gate on anything. Everything ships behind a risk disclosure: no responsibility for anyone's funds, participation is the user's choice.
Risk disclosure page (T-008/T-009) is therefore mandatory before Gen-0 opens. Ticket index entries "+ legal" become "+ risk disclosure".
